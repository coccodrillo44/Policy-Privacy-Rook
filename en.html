2. Scope of Application and Recipients

This Privacy Policy applies to anyone using Rook services, specifically:

1
Guest Users (Unauthenticated Visitors)

Individuals freely exploring the App in anonymous Guest Mode to view the event catalog without creating or linking a user account.

2
Registered Users

Individuals signing in via Google Sign-In or with Email and Password to save and sync favorites, configure notification alerts, write reviews, or chat with organizers.

Organizers and Organization Representatives

Natural persons or representatives of public bodies, local tourist boards (Pro Loco), committees, and associations publishing events, managing co-organizer partnerships, handling chats, or activating promotional boosts.

3. Categories of Personal Data Processed

Depending on how the user interacts with the App, Rook may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

3.1. Authentication and Account Data (Registered Users)

The application enables secure sign-in through certified Firebase Authentication infrastructure via two options:

  • Google Sign-In (Credential Manager): collects a unique alphanumeric identifier (Firebase UID), the email address linked to the Google account, user name/surname, and public avatar URL;
  • Email and Password Sign-In: collects email address and password, the latter protected by irreversible cryptographic hashing managed directly by Firebase Authentication. The Controller never views or stores plaintext passwords;
  • Profile Display Data: public display name or nickname, optional organization name, and profile verification badge status;
  • Staff-Organization Link: unique alphanumeric staff code entered by authorized members to link their personal account to an organization profile, validated securely via Firebase Cloud Functions.

3.2. Preferences, Interests, and Custom Alerts (Keyword Alert)

  • "Favorites" List: event identifiers saved to the user account;
  • Followed Organizers: list of entities and associations followed for updates;
  • Selected Notification Categories: user preferences (e.g. Food Festivals/Sagre, Live Music, Art, Traditional Folklore);
  • Inclusivity & Food Filters: dietary preferences (vegetarian/vegan) and wheelchair accessibility filters;
  • Custom Keyword Alerts: user-defined search terms (e.g. artist names, specific fairs, regional products like truffles or chestnuts, gluten-free options) to trigger automatic push notifications whenever a matching event is published;
  • Consent and Settings Status: configuration settings stored locally and in the cloud user profile.

3.3. Geolocation Data (GPS Position)

Rook can detect the real-time geographic location (latitude and longitude coordinates of the mobile device).

Acquisition conditions and total transparency:
GPS acquisition occurs EXCLUSIVELY subject to explicit consent granted through the mobile operating system dialog ("While using the app" or "Only this time").

Location data is processed exclusively to:

  1. Calculate distance (straight-line or road proximity) to events listed in the catalog;
  2. Sort events dynamically based on geographical proximity to the user's current position;
  3. Display the user's location on the interactive map integrated via Google Maps SDK.
⚠️
GPS PRIVACY GUARANTEE — ZERO LOCATION HISTORY:
GPS data is NEVER permanently saved on the Controller's servers, is NOT used to profile or build location histories, and is NOT tracked in the background when the screen is locked or the app is closed.
If GPS permission is denied, users can still fully utilize the app by manually selecting any Tuscan province or municipality.

3.4. User-Generated Content (Reviews, Chat, and Reports)

  • Public Reviews and Ratings: star rating (1 to 5), review text, publication timestamp, and user display name associated with their UID. Users maintain complete control to edit or delete their reviews at any time directly from the event screen;
  • In-App Chat Messages: real-time text communications between visitors and event organizers via dedicated Firebase Realtime Database channels, used to ask questions regarding event schedules and logistics;
  • Event Reports: reporter's email address, reason for report, descriptive details, ticket processing status, and resolution history by the responsible organizer.

3.5. Push Notification Data (Firebase Cloud Messaging - FCM)

When push notifications are authorized (Android 13+ system permission POST_NOTIFICATIONS), a unique Device Registration Token (FCM Token) is generated.
This token allows Firebase Cloud Functions servers to deliver push alerts matching chosen topics (event categories) or tracked keywords, without tracking external web browsing.

3.6. Organizer Data, Event Listings, and Visibility Boosts

For organizers and representatives uploading event listings:

  • Entity details: official organization name, committee, association, or Pro Loco;
  • Public contact info: official public inquiry email, phone numbers for bookings/info, website links, or social media pages;
  • Descriptive & media content: cover photos, promotional flyers, dates, opening hours, programs, food menus, and price lists;
  • Visibility Boosters: promotional plan requests (24h, 48h, 7 days) storing boost expiration dates for featured positioning;
  • Inter-Entity Collaborations: co-organizer partnership data (sent/received requests, approval status) and reputation score with awarded trust badges.

3.7. Technical, Log, and Diagnostic Data

  • IP Address: processed transiently to route HTTPS network requests;
  • Device Information: device brand, model, and Android OS version;
  • Timestamps and network logs: access time records of requests made to Firebase cloud databases;
  • Diagnostic & crash logs: aggregated error and crash reports used solely to keep the app secure, performant, and stable.

5. Nature of Data Provision

Providing personal data has different legal implications depending on the feature used:

Mandatory Technical
Essential Session & Connection Data: Implicit and technically indispensable for establishing Internet connections. Withholding this data makes delivering the event catalog technically impossible.
Optional / Account
Google or Email/Password Sign-In: Completely optional. Users may browse freely in anonymous Guest Mode. Not registering only restricts cloud Favorites syncing, chat, leaving reviews, and custom alerts.
Optional / GPS
Geolocation Permission (GPS): Optional. Denying GPS access only disables automatic distance calculation and proximity sorting, without impeding manual browsing of Tuscan provinces or viewing events on the map.
Optional / Notifications
Push Notifications & Keyword Alerts: Optional. Denying permission only prevents receiving real-time alerts on newly published events, leaving all other app features fully accessible.

6. Processing Methods and Security Measures

Personal data is processed using electronic, digital, and automated systems in compliance with principles of fairness, lawfulness, transparency, purpose limitation, and data minimization set out in Article 5 GDPR.

Pursuant to Article 32 GDPR, the Controller has implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect data against accidental loss, unlawful use, unauthorized access, or unauthorized alteration:

🔒 Encryption in Transit (HTTPS/TLS)

All communications between the mobile client app, APIs, and Firebase cloud servers pass through encrypted channels using advanced TLS/HTTPS security protocols.

🛡️ Firebase Security Rules

Granular server-side security rules ensure complete data segregation: authenticated users can access and modify only their own favorites, reviews, and settings linked to their UID.

🤖 On-Device Translation (Google ML Kit)

Real-time multilingual translations (Italian, English, German) are processed locally on the smartphone using downloaded on-device machine learning models, ensuring viewed texts are never sent to third-party profiling servers.

🔑 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Administrative access to Firebase management consoles, storage buckets, and source code repositories is strictly protected by mandatory multi-factor authentication restricted to the Controller.

7. Data Retention Policy

Personal data is retained only for as long as strictly necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, specifically:

👤

User Account, Favorites & Keywords

Profile data, favorites, and saved keywords are retained for the active duration of the account, or until self-deleted by the user. Accounts inactive for over 24 consecutive months may be permanently deleted.

⭐

Reviews and Ratings

Reviews remain published on event listings until edited or deleted by the user in-app, or until the user account or the event itself is removed.

💬

In-App Chat Messages

Messages exchanged between visitors and organizers are retained for the duration of the event inquiries, and can be deleted upon request or after the event concludes.

📍

GPS Geolocation

Location data is processed exclusively in volatile device memory in real time. No location data is ever saved, logged, or historized on the Controller's servers.

🔔

Push Notification Tokens (FCM)

Device tokens are retained until notifications are disabled, system permissions are revoked, or the app is uninstalled.

📋

Technical & Diagnostic Logs

Connection logs and IP addresses are retained for a maximum of 12 months for IT security and auditing purposes.

10. Data Subject Rights (Articles 15-22 GDPR)

Users are entitled at any time to exercise the rights granted by Articles 15 through 22 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679:

Right of Access (Art. 15)

Obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed, and receive an intelligible copy thereof.

Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

Request without undue delay the correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

Right to Erasure / Oblivion (Art. 17)

Obtain the prompt and permanent deletion of your personal data from databases and backup archives.

Right to Restriction (Art. 18)

Request the temporary limitation of data processing under circumstances established by law.

Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (JSON).

Right to Object (Art. 21)

Object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests.

Revocation of Consent

Withdraw previously granted consent (e.g. GPS or notifications) at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

10.1. Account & Data Deletion Procedure (Google Play Compliance & Art. 17 GDPR)

⚡
Account Deletion Request for Rook (Developer: TFDLab):
In compliance with Google Play Developer policies and GDPR erasure rights (Art. 17), users can request permanent account and associated data deletion via two methods:
  • Method 1 — Directly In-App (Recommended):
    1. Open the Rook application on your Android device;
    2. Navigate to the «Profile» section via the bottom navigation bar;
    3. Scroll down and tap the «Delete Account» button;
    4. Confirm the request in the confirmation dialog to initiate immediate deletion.
  • Method 2 — Web / Email Request (Without reinstalling the app):
    Send an email to the Data Controller at tfdlab.info@gmail.com with the subject line "Rook Account Deletion Request", specifying the email address associated with your Google or app account. The Controller will execute permanent deletion within 30 days and send confirmation.
Data deleted: Firebase UID, email address, profile name/nickname, favorites list, followed organizers, saved keyword alerts, device FCM tokens, and authored reviews.
Retention period: No personal data is retained (additional retention: 0 days). Deletion from Firebase Auth and databases is immediate, total, and irreversible.

10.2. Autonomous In-App Management of Reviews and Notifications

Users have autonomous, direct control over their content:
- Reviews: edit or delete directly from the event screen where posted;
- Notifications & Keyword Alerts: activate, silence, or modify anytime from the Notifications card in Profile.

10.3. Exercising Rights via Email

For any data request, contact the Data Controller at tfdlab.info@gmail.com. We will respond without undue delay within 30 days.
